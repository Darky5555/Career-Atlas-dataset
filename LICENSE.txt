INDICATION OF CHANGES

Required by CC BY. The licensed inputs were transformed rather than
redistributed as-is: joined to 2018 SOC codes, reconciled across the 2018 and
2019 SOC revisions, rescaled to common ranges, aggregated from O*NET-SOC
sub-codes to 6-digit codes, and combined into the derived and modeled columns
described in data-dictionary.csv.

WHAT THE LICENCE DOES NOT COVER

Attribution is a legal requirement. The following is not, and is asked for
anyway: the columns marked "modeled" in the data dictionary are our judgement,
not measurement, and there is no ground-truth dataset of AI displacement to
check them against. If you republish those columns, please carry that caveat
with them.
